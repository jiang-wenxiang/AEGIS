# AEGIS 



## AEGIS: Attentive Embedding via Global Image Spaces for Robust Watermarking



Mingzhu Zhou, Shuo Zhao, Deze Yang, Yawen Lin, Wenxiang Jiang

[[PDF]](\) 

### Abstract 
Deep image watermarking plays a pivotal role in copyright protection. Recent deep-learning-based watermarking methods have made significant strides in imperceptibility and robustness against signal processing transforms. However, their performance in addressing non-linear geometric distortions (e.g., perspective warping, rotation) remains suboptimal, a limitation primarily stemming from the inherent dependence of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) on pixel grid alignment. Furthermore, a single feature domain often struggles to simultaneously defend against diverse types of attacks. To bridge this gap, we propose Attentive Embedding network via Global Image Spaces (AEGIS). The framework integrates features across four transform domains—Spatial, Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT), Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT), and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)—while leveraging a multi-head residual attention mechanism to adaptively weight these diverse representations. Our ablation studies demonstrate that these transform domains play complementary roles in watermark embedding: the SVD domain preserves global structural consistency to ensure visual quality; the DWT and spatial domains enhance stability against geometric transformations; and the DCT domain provides fine-grained adjustments. Experimental results indicate that AEGIS performs comparably to state-of-the-art methods under conventional signal noise and occlusion. Notably, AEGIS exhibits superior robustness against perspective warping and rotation distortions that are highly prevalent in real-world scenarios. This suggests that AEGIS offers an effective approach to mitigating geometric distortion challenges in physical-world applications.
